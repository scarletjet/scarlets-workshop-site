How it works

Four moves, one tap

A shared link becomes a written guide in a single pass — no manual transcribing, no copy-paste gymnastics.

01

Share a link

Paste it, or tap Share in TikTok / Instagram and pick the app straight from the Android share sheet.

02

Pull the audio

yt-dlp + ffmpeg grab just the spoken track and shrink it to a small mono clip.

03

Understand it

One call to Google Gemini listens to the audio and writes the title, materials, and ordered steps.

04

Keep it

Auto-tagged by topic and saved to your history, ready to reopen, filter, or copy.

What it does

Features in hand today

Public links from both platforms, pasted or shared.

Organise

Output

One-tap copy of the whole guide, plus the raw transcript on demand.

Guardrail

10-minute cap

Long videos are turned away before download to keep it fast and cheap.

Under the hood

Built lean on purpose

Backend
Python · FastAPI
Fetch
yt-dlp + ffmpeg + Deno
Intelligence
OpenRouter → Gemini 2.5 Flash — a single audio call does transcription and step-writing
Storage
SQLite (topic-tagged history)
Frontend
Installable PWA — plain HTML / CSS / JS, no framework
Ship
Docker on Render · source on GitHub (private)
The frugal trick: instead of a paid transcription service plus a separate writing model, one audio-capable model does both jobs in a single call — pennies per video, on one API key.
